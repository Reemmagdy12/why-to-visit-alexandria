Why to visit Alexandria ?

History Bibliotheca Alexandrina Mediterranean Atmosphere

Alexandria, Egypt’s "Mediterranean Jewel," offers a captivating mix of ancient history, Mediterranean charm, and coastal relaxation, making it an essential, lower-key alternative to Cairo or Luxor. It is the perfect destination for history lovers seeking Greco-Roman treasures, foodies craving fresh seafood, and travelers wishing to stroll a vibrant seaside Corniche.

A Living Bridge Between Ancient History and Modernity

Unlike museums where history is behind glass, Alexandria is a living museum. Founded by Alexander the Great, the city allows you to walk through the same streets that echoed with the knowledge of ancient scholars. Key historical sites include the awe-inspiring Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa, an architectural blend of Pharaonic, Hellenic, and Roman styles, and the Roman Amphitheatre.

It is a living museum, where the ancient comfortably coexists with the brand-new

The Rebirth of Knowledge at the Bibliotheca Alexandrina

The modern Bibliotheca Alexandrina is not merely a library; it is a massive cultural and scientific complex designed to revive the legacy of the Ancient Library of Alexandria. With its stunning sun-dial shape, it contains millions of books, museums, and planetariums, making it a "must-visit" for researchers, history enthusiasts, and anyone seeking cultural immersion.

If you seek a destination that combines intellectual history with sun-savvy, casual relaxation, Alexandria is unparalleled in Egypt. As one visitor noted, "By the time you have to leave, you'll wish that you would've stayed longer".

Mediterranean Atmosphere and Culinary Delights

Alexandria offers a relaxed, laid-back Mediterranean vibe far removed from the intensity of Cairo, featuring a 19km long seaside promenade called the Corniche. You can spend your days wandering the 15th-century Qaitbay Citadel—built on the original site of the Lighthouse of Alexandria—and your evenings dining on world-class fresh seafood in the Greek Quarter or near the harbor.

It’s a city steeped in history and legend, and can easily hold its own against Cairo
